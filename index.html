The Bauhaus

A German art school that ran for fourteen years, was shut down by politics, and still decides what most of the things around you look like.

Below is the Dessau building, designed by Walter Gropius in 1925. Steel frame, glass curtain wall, and the name set vertically in sans serif down the corner.
